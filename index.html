About Our Clinic

Restoring Function. Relieving Discomfort. Lasting Results.

At Neuro Massage Therapy, we focus on restoring muscle function and treating both acute and chronic dysfunction, bringing the body into homeostasis and supporting the long-term health of every client we serve.

Based on the St. John Method of Neuromuscular Therapy, we treat a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions — from chronic neck pain, TMJ dysfunction, headaches and migraines, to shoulder pain, lower back pain, hip pain, sciatica, plantar fasciitis and referred pain throughout the body. If you are experiencing discomfort anywhere in your body, chances are we can help.

We treat the body as a unified whole — because true healing begins when we address the root cause, not just where it hurts.

What We Offer

Our Specialized Treatments

Each modality we offer is carefully selected for its ability to produce real, measurable results in pain relief, function, and recovery.

01

Neuromuscular Therapy

NMT · Trigger Points

A specialized, hands-on approach that brings balance to the nervous and musculoskeletal systems. We focus on the true source of dysfunction — identifying and releasing trigger points, decompressing nerves, and restoring the body's natural movement patterns for lasting relief.

02

Cupping Therapy

Myofascial Decompression

Using the decompression technique, cupping lifts and separates the fascia from the underlying layers of skin and tissue. This creates space within the soft tissue, promoting circulation, releasing adhesions, and accelerating the body's natural healing process — a powerful complement to neuromuscular therapy.

03

Dolphin Neurostim

Microcurrent Point Stimulation

The Dolphin Neurostim applies concentrated microcurrent stimulation to specific points on the body, combining the principles of acupuncture, osteopathy, and neurology. It works by calming the nervous system, releasing chronic muscle tension, and reducing scar tissue — providing fast, lasting pain relief without needles or medication.

Irma

Certified Neuromuscular Therapist, CNMT — St. John Method

Irma founded Neuro Massage Therapy out of a deep passion for helping people who live with the onset and constant pain in their bodies. She believes that everyone deserves to move freely, feel well, and live without the burden of chronic discomfort.

Upon graduating as a Licensed Massage Therapist from Florida Metropolitan University (FMU), Irma immediately joined a Neuromuscular Therapy clinic specializing in the St. John Method, where she spent two years refining her skills alongside seasoned practitioners.
